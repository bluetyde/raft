# pwr-beavrs-hzp-openmc

A PWR at **hot zero power** (566.5 K, 15.5 MPa water, no decay heat), built with OpenMC 0.15.3 and ENDF/B-VIII.0 from the BEAVRS benchmark cycle 1 model (MIT, `mit-crpg/BEAVRS`). Status `experimental`. Exporter: `python -m pipeline.library.export_beavrs`; sweep: `pipeline/openmc/beavrs_sweep.py` (45 cases); plan: `docs/m8-beavrs-exporter-plan.md`; comparison with the measured hot zero power data: `docs/validation/m8-beavrs-hzp-results.md`.

**Use it for zero-power physics only** (the shipped lessons are approach to criticality by dilution, `web/src/app/scenarios/pwr-beavrs-hzp-dilution.json`, near the benchmark rod configuration with all banks out and bank D at step 213, and by bank withdrawal, `pwr-beavrs-hzp-bank-withdrawal.json`, with banks D and C in, the others out and 900 ppm of boron). Soluble boron works there, and the critical concentration is 983 ppm in both engines. With several banks in, the library is checked only at the states listed in `domain.jointChecked` (below). A trip scrams the rods at about 1.7 MW (0.05 % of full power).

## What comes from transport (source `transport`)

- Reactivity tables for nine banks (A, B, C, D, SA to SE; ids are lowercase, `a` to `se`), fuel temperature, coolant temperature (water temperature and density together) and boron; the reference is the benchmark configuration (bank D at step 213 of 228, all others out) at 975 ppm, where the model is at +101 pcm (kept as it is, no calibration).
- Delayed-neutron fractions (sum 700.7 pcm) and the generation time (29.3 µs) from an iterated-fission-probability run.
- Fission power shapes: 193 assemblies by 10 axial bins; one delta array per bank and one for fuel temperature. The shape does not change with coolant temperature or boron.

## What is assumed (source `assumed`)

Rod speed (72 steps/min) and the scram profile (about 2 s); fuel heat capacity; coolant mass (260 t) and heat capacity; fuel to coolant heat transfer (8.5 MW/K); the steam generator (its sink is the reference temperature, so it removes nothing at zero power); poison data; the neutron source (2 W/s); the instrument ranges, lags and noise; the trip set points (109 % of 3411 MW, 1.7 MW on the log channel, a 10 s period); boron range (0 to 2000 ppm) and rate (0.1 ppm/s). The fuel mass (93 t) is computed from the benchmark's pellet size and a density of 10.3 g/cm3. Delayed-group decay constants are the ENDF/B-VIII.0 values for U-235, not Keepin's.

## Known limits

- **Temperature feedback is not validated**: the model's isothermal temperature coefficient is 1.6 to 2.0 pcm/°F too negative against the benchmark measurement.
- **Compared with the measured physics tests** (fifteenth protocol, `docs/validation/m8-rod-shadowing-results.md`): critical boron is within 50 ppm at all five measured states (+8, +22, +24, +15 and +10 ppm with all rods out, D in, C and D in, A to D in, seven banks in); of the seven sequential bank worths four are good (D, C, SC and, as run, SD), three are plausible or fail (B, A and SE, +12 %, -31 % and +25 %) and the total is 5993 pcm against 6042 measured; the temperature coefficient fails at both states (-3.6 pcm/°F against -1.75 and -8.01), and is the same whatever the rods. The critical-boron results at C and D in, A to D in and seven banks in come partly from the cases the rod model was fitted to.
- **Rod banks are not additive; the library corrects for it.** Each bank's worth is scaled by 1 + g(the summed reactivity of the other banks) (`reactivity.rodShadowing`, capability `rod-interaction-v2`, `docs/library-format.md` section 17), fitted to twelve transport cases with several banks in and to nothing else. Against 13 further transport cases it was held out from, nine are within the limit (100 pcm or three standard errors) or plausible; the library is within 100 pcm at 4 of 5 states with seven banks in and C part-way (at 860 ppm; +268 pcm at C = 40 steps), and within 110 pcm at 3 of 4 states of another insertion order (-284 pcm at the fourth); with SA and SB in (not in the fit) it is off by +560 and +700 pcm at two states. `domain.jointChecked` lists the 13 good states (each carries a boron window of ±10 ppm around the concentration it was run at: 860 ppm for five (seven banks in with C part-way, and all rods out), 900 ppm for five (D in and C at 0, 80, 140, 180 and 228 steps), 975 ppm for three; at another boron the state is `unvalidated`); any other state with two or more banks away from the reference is `unvalidated`, and a bank's surroundings beyond the table (about -3850 pcm of the other banks) are `outOfDomain`. The shapes of the summed deltas also go negative in some bins with several banks in (`pipeline/openmc/results/beavrs_export_report.json`). The boron x bank interaction (about 230 and 330 pcm at the two states with boron changed) is not in the model.
- Xenon worth is zero (not computed); there is no decay heat and no pressure; the library has no pulse mode.
- The 3D model shows the vessel barrel, 193 assemblies and 53 absorber assemblies as boxes and cylinders, not the pins.
